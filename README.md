# Chatty vs Tidio vs Zipchat vs Gorgias: AI chat apps for Shopify compared (2026)

Four Shopify apps that put an AI agent in your store chat, each built for a different job:

- **Chatty:** an AI sales agent and live chat app for Shopify stores, with a free plan.
- **Tidio:** live chat, a helpdesk and the Lyro AI agent, for Shopify and many other website platforms.
- **Zipchat:** an AI sales agent for ecommerce brands that answers on chat, email and social. It says it is not a helpdesk.
- **Gorgias:** an ecommerce helpdesk, with AI Agent sold as a paid add-on.

Last verified: November 20, 2026

Maintained by the Chatty team (Avada). Every cell links to the vendor's own page. Corrections welcome via issues or PRs.

## Which one fits your store

These notes describe fit, based only on the data in this repo. They are not rankings.

### Pick Chatty if

- **Platform:** you sell on Shopify. Chatty has no native app for other ecommerce platforms.
- **Budget:** you want a free plan with AI (50 AI conversations a month) or a paid plan from $19.99 a month.
- **Proof of sales:** you want attributed orders marked in Shopify admin. Chatty writes order metafields you can filter on.
- **Setup:** you want the vendor to set up and train the AI for you, at no charge, on any plan.
- **Know before you pick:** AI replies on email, Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp are on Pro and Plus only. There is no SMS or voice channel. The AI routes return and refund requests to your team; it does not issue refunds. Ticketing is in early access.

### Pick Tidio if

- **Platform:** you run on Shopify and other platforms, such as WooCommerce, BigCommerce or a custom site.
- **Channels:** you want Lyro AI on chat, email, Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp on any plan that includes Lyro.
- **Billing:** you want one conversation quota shared by AI and team, with no overage. At the limit, replies stop until renewal or upgrade, unless you turn on auto-upgrade.
- **Helpdesk:** you want ticketing included on all plans.
- **Know before you pick:** sales reports cover Shopify stores only, and the full Sales section is on Growth and up. Lyro gets sales credit only when a shopper clicks a Lyro product card before buying. These plans apply to accounts that subscribed on or after November 16, 2026.

### Pick Zipchat if

- **Volume:** you expect high AI reply volume. Plans go from 750 to 14,000 replies a month, and an Unlimited plan starts at $2,100 a month.
- **Channels:** you want AI on chat, email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and social comments on every plan.
- **Team:** you want unlimited team seats on every plan.
- **Measuring lift:** you want a built-in A/B test to compare sales with and without the AI.
- **Know before you pick:** the entry price on the website is $80 a month; the free plan appears only on the Shopify App Store listing. Zipchat is not a helpdesk and has no mobile app. Add to cart works on Shopify only.

### Pick Gorgias if

- **Helpdesk:** you need a full helpdesk with rules, macros, SLA policies and order edits inside tickets.
- **AI actions:** you want the AI to cancel orders, refund, reship or edit addresses (Actions, Basic plan and up).
- **Channels:** you want voice and SMS as paid add-ons in the same inbox.
- **Platform:** your helpdesk also needs to cover WooCommerce, BigCommerce or Magento stores.
- **Know before you pick:** there is no free plan. AI Agent is a paid add-on. Each AI-resolved ticket pays a helpdesk ticket fee plus an automated interaction fee. The revenue report is on Pro and up.

## How each app counts sales from chat

The four apps use different rules to decide which orders came from chat. Windows, triggers and plan limits differ, so the revenue numbers they report are not comparable across apps. None of these methods measures incremental lift on its own.

**Chatty:** an order counts as Chatty Assisted when the customer chatted with Chatty (AI or team) or viewed the store's FAQs within 7 days before ordering. This is attribution, not incremental lift. The help center says: "The card shows a link between chat and sales, not the lift chat caused." Chatty writes order metafields (Chatty Assisted, Chatty Direct, Chatty Conversation, Chatty Origin), so merchants can filter and check these orders in Shopify admin. The Chat-to-Sales report is on Basic and up. Sources: [attribution metafields](https://help.chatty.net/analytics/Chatty-Attribution-Metafields), [Chat-to-Sales](https://help.chatty.net/analytics/Chat-to-Sales).

**Tidio:** Tidio counts paid Shopify orders only. Lyro gets credit when the shopper clicked a Lyro product recommendation before buying; no time window is stated. Flows get credit when the shopper interacted with a Flow product recommendation or coupon. Human agents get credit for orders within 7 days after the chat. The Sales assisted overview is on every plan, and the full Sales section is on Growth and up. Non-Shopify projects see click metrics only. Source: [conversion rate](https://help.tidio.com/articles/conversion-rate/).

**Zipchat:** Zipchat uses last touch within a fixed 24-hour window. On the store, a purchase counts if it happens within 24 hours of the last message. On WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, email and comments, an order counts if it happens within 24 hours of clicking a link the agent sent. The AI then rates each order's influence. Refunds do not remove attributed orders. The docs say: "It does not prove that the conversation alone caused the sale." Zipchat also offers a built-in A/B test on every plan. Source: [tracking and attribution](https://docs.zipchat.ai/settings-tracking-and-security/tracking-and-attribution).

**Gorgias:** Gorgias uses a 3-day window. Shopping Assistant counts an order placed within 3 days of its last message, and drops it if the conversation was handed to a human. The helpdesk Revenue report counts a purchase within 3 days of ticket creation from the same email, and removes fully refunded orders. The Revenue report is on Pro, Advanced and Enterprise. Sources: [how metrics are calculated](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-metrics-are-calculated-ai-and-automation-5666070), [revenue statistics](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/revenue-statistics-81744).
